Services Offered

We provide comprehensive repair and maintenance for all types of vehicles and motorcycles.

Engine Diagnostics

Advanced computer diagnostics to find exactly what's wrong with your engine. We use state-of-the-art equipment to identify issues quickly and accurately.

Motorcycle Repair

Specialized care for all motorcycle brands, from oil changes to full engine rebuilds. Our expert technicians handle everything from routine maintenance to complex repairs.

Brake & Suspension

About AutoCare

Founded in 2005, AutoCare has grown from a small local garage to the city's most trusted auto and motor repair shop. Our team consists of certified admins, dedicated cashiers, skilled staff, and we treat every client like family.


We believe in transparency, quality parts, and honest labor. Whether you drive a compact car or ride a heavy-duty motorcycle, we have the tools and expertise to get you back on the road safely.
