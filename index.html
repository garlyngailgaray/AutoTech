About AutoCare

Founded in 2005, AutoCare has grown from a small local garage to the city's most trusted auto and motor repair shop. Our team consists of certified admins, dedicated cashiers, skilled staff, and we treat every client like family.

We believe in transparency, quality parts, and honest labor. Whether you drive a compact car or ride a heavy-duty motorcycle, we have the tools and expertise to get you back on the road safely.
